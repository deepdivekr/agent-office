# Agent Office v0.8.12

- **Schedule and delivery conditions are Office's.** A Work defined before v0.8.9 may still carry a condition such as "the daily schedule is active". In a new conversation (v0.8.11) the AI news Work read it as its own task, marked it unmet, stopped for review, and proposed changing its hourly schedule back to daily 09:00. Every run's prompt, full or short, now says that such a condition is Office's: the AI marks it met for Office to check with its own records and does not save `SCHEDULE.json` for it. `SCHEDULE.json` is only for a cadence the owner asks for or the work clearly needs.

## Upgrade

Finish active work, close MCP clients and the Control Center, and rerun the installer:

```bash
bash -o pipefail -c 'curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/deepdivekr/agent-office/v0.8.12/install.sh | bash'
```

Saved Works, schedules and settings carry over. The install verifier upgrades from v0.3.1 through v0.8.11.
